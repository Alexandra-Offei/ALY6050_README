# ALY 6050 — Introduction to Enterprise Analytics

**Northeastern University · Roux Institute**  
**Course:** ALY 6050 Introduction to Enterprise Analytics  
**Tools:** R · Excel · Solver · Linear Programming

---

## Overview

Quantitative decision-making methods for business, covering probability, simulation, forecasting, inventory optimisation, linear programming, and network optimisation. Projects applied prescriptive and predictive analytics to real operational problems — directly aligned with 16+ years of supply chain experience.

---

## Projects

### Module 1 — Probability & Simulation
**Scenario:** Boston Red Sox vs New York Yankees best-of-three series

- Probability tree construction for series outcomes
- Betting outcome simulation: +$500 win / −$520 loss per game
- Expected value calculation across all series scenarios
- Three-part structured analysis of probabilistic outcomes

---

### Module 2 — Decision Analysis & Monte Carlo Simulation
**Scenario:** JET Corporation dam construction investment decision

- Benefit-cost analysis for two dam projects (Southwest Georgia vs North Carolina)
- **Monte Carlo simulation** for probabilistic evaluation under uncertainty
- Decision tree construction with expected monetary values
- Structured investment recommendation based on simulated distributions

---

### Module 3 — Financial Time Series Forecasting ⭐
**Stocks:** Apple (AAPL) · Honeywell (HON)

**Short-term forecasting:**
- Line plotting and trend identification
- Exponential smoothing with MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error) calculation

**Long-term forecasting:**
- Weighted moving averages
- Linear trend extrapolation
- Comparison of forecasted vs actual stock values

**Regression analysis:**
- Regression model for stock value prediction
- Residual analysis for model appropriateness assessment
- **Portfolio allocation recommendation** between AAPL and HON

---

### Module 4 — Inventory Optimisation: EOQ Model ⭐
**Scenario:** Manufacturing company — key engine component inventory

- **Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) model** to minimise total inventory costs
- Trade-off analysis: holding costs vs ordering costs
- Optimal order quantity calculation
- Safety stock and reorder point determination
- Direct application of supply chain domain expertise

---

### Module 5 — Linear Programming: Profit Maximisation ⭐
**Scenario:** Northern Hardware Company — new Southeast distribution centre

**Products:** Pressure washers · go-karts · generators · water pumps

**Constraints:**
- Monthly purchasing budget: $170,000
- Warehouse space: 82 shelves with defined dimensions
- Varying storage requirements per product

**Analysis:**
- LP model formulation with decision variables, objective function, and constraints
- Excel Solver for optimal product mix
- Sensitivity analysis: shadow prices and ranging
- Maximum profit allocation recommendation

---

### Module 6 — Network Optimisation & Risk Minimisation ⭐
**Scenario:** Rockhill Shipping & Transfer Company — hazardous chemical waste routing

**Problem A — Transshipment:**
- 6 manufacturing plants → 3 disposal facilities
- Minimum cost shipping network design
- Node and arc formulation
- Excel Solver for optimal routing

**Problem B — Risk Minimisation:**
- Multi-objective optimisation balancing cost and transportation risk
- Risk-weighted routing for hazardous materials
- Trade-off between cost efficiency and safety compliance
